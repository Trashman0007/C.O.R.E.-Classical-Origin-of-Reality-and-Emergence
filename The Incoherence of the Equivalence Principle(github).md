### Abstract

Nature is spatially a flat Euclidean 3-space. Newton’s laws encode an absolute distinction: velocity is relative while acceleration is absolute. General Relativity (GR) erases this distinction via the Equivalence Principle (EP). We demonstrate that the EP is false by carefully distinguishing three physically different situations in flat Euclidean space: (1) pure relative motion at constant velocity, (2) pure laboratory acceleration with no vacuum strain, and (3) real gravity that strains the vacuum permittivity and permeability. Starting from phase continuity of continuous electromagnetic waves and the impedance-invariant CUGE vacuum response, we derive the ray equation. Under pure acceleration the vacuum refractive index remains $n \equiv 1$; the only effects are ordinary special-relativistic Doppler shifts (first-order longitudinal or second-order transverse). Under real gravity the vacuum is strained ($n > 1$), atomic clocks and rulers co-scale with $\varepsilon$ and $\mu$, and an additional refractive half appears. The clean experimental signature is the directional half-effect observed by Sturm (2022): pure horizontal acceleration produces only the kinematic (transverse Doppler) half, while real gravity always produces the full shift. GR’s EP cannot produce a directional half under pure acceleration. The Equivalence Principle is therefore false, and the geometric interpretation of gravity is unnecessary. All derivations use SI base units, dimensionless refractive index, and flat Euclidean geometry only.

**Keywords:** Equivalence Principle, acceleration vs motion vs gravity, half-effect, refractive vacuum, longitudinal Doppler, transverse Doppler, C.O.R.E., CUGE, REFORM, flat Euclidean space

### 1. Introduction: Three Distinct Physical Situations

In flat Euclidean space the geometry of nature is ordinary. Within this arena three situations must be carefully distinguished:

1. **Pure relative motion** at constant velocity (no acceleration, no mass nearby). Only special-relativistic kinematic effects appear (transverse Doppler, time dilation).

2. **Pure laboratory acceleration** (external forces accelerate the apparatus; vacuum remains unstrained, $n \equiv 1$). Ordinary Doppler shifts of special relativity appear according to the geometry of the light path relative to the acceleration vector. No refractive contribution exists because $\varepsilon$ and $\mu$ are unchanged.

3. **Real gravity** (mass present, vacuum strained: $\varepsilon$ and $\mu$ increase symmetrically). Both the kinematic contribution and a genuine refractive contribution from the changed vacuum properties appear. Atomic clocks and rulers co-scale with the local medium.

General Relativity’s Equivalence Principle asserts that situation (2) is locally indistinguishable from situation (3). We prove this is false. The proof rests on the correct special-relativistic treatment of Doppler shifts under pure acceleration together with the additional physical effect (vacuum strain + co-scaling of standards) that only real gravity produces.

A previous draft contained an error in the pure-acceleration vertical case by incorrectly substituting the transverse Doppler formula. That error is corrected here. The directional half-effect and the co-scaling distinction survive the correction and remain decisive.

### 2. Newton’s Absolute Acceleration in Flat Euclidean Space

Under a Galilean (or Lorentz) boost, velocity transforms while acceleration does not. The four-acceleration magnitude is a Lorentz scalar. Absolute acceleration is therefore a frame-independent physical fact. Any theory that treats pure acceleration as interchangeable with a real gravitational field is already in tension with this elementary property of the transformation laws.

### 3. The Standard Elevator Argument and Its Limitation

The classic Einstein elevator argument proceeds as follows. A rocket has proper acceleration $a$. A photon is emitted from the floor toward a receiver at height $L$. Work in the momentarily-comoving inertial frame at emission. The photon transit time is $t \approx L/c$. In that interval the receiver acquires an additional velocity

$$
v = a t \approx \frac{a L}{c}
$$

in the direction of propagation (the receiver is “running away”). This produces a first-order longitudinal Doppler shift

$$
\frac{\Delta f}{f} = -\frac{v}{c} = -\frac{a L}{c^2}.
$$

For $g = a$ this numerically equals the gravitational redshift. The argument is correct as far as it goes: under pure acceleration there is indeed a first-order longitudinal Doppler contribution when the light path is parallel to the acceleration.

**The limitation of the argument** is that it only accounts for the kinematic Doppler effect of relative motion during flight. It says nothing about whether the vacuum itself is altered, whether atomic standards co-scale, or what happens when the light path is transverse to the acceleration. Those distinctions are decisive.

### 4. Correct First-Principles Derivation in Flat Euclidean Space

We work in ordinary Euclidean 3-space. The sole physical requirements are phase continuity of continuous electromagnetic waves and the impedance-invariant vacuum response of CUGE:

$$
\varepsilon(r) = \varepsilon_0\left(1 + \frac{\Phi}{2c^2}\right), \qquad
\mu(r) = \mu_0\left(1 + \frac{\Phi}{2c^2}\right),
$$

yielding the dimensionless refractive index

$$
n(r) \equiv \sqrt{\varepsilon_r \mu_r} \approx 1 + \frac{\Phi}{2c^2}.
$$

#### 4.1 The Ray Equation

Fermat’s principle $\delta \int n\, dl = 0$ yields the ray equation. In coordinate time:

$$
\frac{d\mathbf{v}}{dt} = \frac{c^2}{n}\nabla n - \frac{\dot{n}}{n}\mathbf{v}.
$$

The first term is the refractive force (present only when the vacuum is strained). The second term is kinematic damping associated with any change of $n$ along the path.

#### 4.2 The Three Cases — Corrected

**Case 1 — Pure relative motion (constant velocity, $n \equiv 1$).**  
No acceleration, no mass. Only special-relativistic kinematic effects: transverse Doppler (second-order) when the light path is perpendicular to the relative velocity, and ordinary first-order Doppler when there is a line-of-sight component. No refractive contribution.

**Case 2 — Pure laboratory acceleration ($n \equiv 1$, vacuum unstrained).**  
External forces accelerate the apparatus; $\varepsilon$ and $\mu$ remain exactly free-space values. Consequently $\nabla n = 0$ and the refractive term vanishes. The only frequency shifts are ordinary special-relativistic Doppler shifts whose form depends on geometry:

- **(a) Vertical / longitudinal (light path parallel to $a$):** During the photon transit time $t \approx L/c$ the receiver acquires velocity $v = a t \approx a L / c$. This produces a first-order longitudinal Doppler shift

$$
\left.\frac{\Delta f}{f}\right|_{\rm long} = -\frac{v}{c} = -\frac{a L}{c^2}.
$$

There is also a smaller second-order transverse contribution of order $(v/c)^2$, but the leading term is first-order and numerically equals the full gravitational value.

- **(b) Horizontal / transverse (light path perpendicular to $a$):** There is no first-order longitudinal Doppler (no line-of-sight velocity component develops). The only effect is the pure kinematic transverse Doppler (time-dilation) term

$$
\left.\frac{\Delta f}{f}\right|_{\rm trans} = -\frac12\frac{v^2}{c^2} \approx -\frac12\frac{a L}{c^2}
$$

(to leading order).

**Crucial point:** Under pure acceleration the vacuum is not strained, so atomic clocks and rulers do not co-scale with any changed $\varepsilon$. The shifts above are purely kinematic (special-relativistic) effects of relative motion. There is no additional refractive contribution from the medium itself.

**Case 3 — Real gravity (vacuum strained, $n > 1$).**  
A real mass produces $\Phi > 0$ and therefore

$$
n = 1 + \frac{\Phi}{2c^2} > 1, \qquad \nabla n \ne 0.
$$

Two physically distinct contributions now appear:

- **Kinematic half:** The same special-relativistic time-dilation / transverse-Doppler contribution that appears under pure acceleration, of order $-\frac12 \Phi/c^2$.

- **Refractive half:** Because $\varepsilon$ and $\mu$ have increased, atomic transition energies scale as $1/\varepsilon^2$ and Bohr radii (rulers) scale as $\varepsilon$. Local clocks therefore run slower and rulers expand. This co-scaling, together with the optical-path delay from $n > 1$, supplies an additional $-\frac12 \Phi/c^2$. The total measured redshift is

$$
\left.\frac{\Delta f}{f}\right|_{\rm grav} = -\frac{\Phi}{c^2} = -\frac{g L}{c^2}.
$$

Moreover, because both $\varepsilon$ and $\mu$ respond symmetrically, the vacuum impedance remains invariant and local Maxwell structure is preserved. This co-scaling mechanism has no counterpart under pure laboratory acceleration, where $\varepsilon$ and $\mu$ are unchanged.

### 5. Why Pure Acceleration and Real Gravity Remain Distinct

Even though the vertical pure-acceleration case produces a first-order Doppler shift that numerically equals $-aL/c^2$, the physical mechanisms are not the same:

1. **Vacuum state:** Under pure acceleration $n \equiv 1$ and $\nabla n = 0$. Under real gravity $n > 1$ and $\nabla n \ne 0$. The ray equation therefore contains an extra force term only in gravity.

2. **Co-scaling of standards:** Atomic clocks and rulers co-scale with $\varepsilon$ and $\mu$ only when the vacuum is actually strained. Under pure acceleration they do not. Local $c$-invariance is preserved in gravity by this co-scaling; under pure acceleration it is preserved simply because nothing has changed.

3. **Directional signature:** Pure acceleration produces a strong directional dependence: full first-order shift when light is parallel to $a$, only the second-order half when light is perpendicular to $a$. Real gravity is isotropic at leading order (the refractive contribution does not care about the orientation of the light path relative to an “acceleration vector”).

4. **Origin of the shift:** In pure acceleration the vertical shift is a pure Doppler effect of relative motion during photon flight. In gravity the full shift is the sum of a kinematic half and a genuine medium-response (refractive) half.

These differences are local and in principle measurable. They constitute a direct violation of the claim that pure acceleration is locally indistinguishable from gravity.

### 6. The Directional Half-Effect: Clean Experimental Signature

The cleanest experimental discriminator is the horizontal (transverse) geometry under pure acceleration. In that orientation the first-order longitudinal Doppler is absent by construction. Only the pure kinematic transverse Doppler survives:

$$
\left.\frac{\Delta f}{f}\right|_{\rm horizontal,\ pure\ accel} = -\frac12\frac{v^2}{c^2} \approx -\frac12\frac{a L}{c^2}.
$$

Real gravity always produces the full $-gL/c^2$ regardless of orientation. Therefore a pure horizontal acceleration experiment that measures only half the gravitational value directly falsifies local equivalence.

This is precisely the directional result reported by Sturm (2022): vertical pure acceleration yields a numerically full shift (first-order Doppler), while horizontal pure acceleration yields half (transverse Doppler only). GR’s Equivalence Principle has no mechanism that can generate a directional factor-of-two difference under pure acceleration; it demands full local equivalence in every orientation.

The half-effect is therefore not an artifact of an incorrect Doppler formula; it is the expected special-relativistic transverse signature once the vacuum remains unstrained. It is a genuine, local, directional distinction between pure acceleration and real gravity.

### 7. Precise Statement of the Incoherence

**Theorem.** In flat Euclidean space the local physics of pure laboratory acceleration is inequivalent to the local physics of a real gravitational field of equal strength. The Equivalence Principle is therefore false.

**Proof (directional form).**  
Consider two laboratories of height $L$:

- **A:** pure acceleration $a$, light path horizontal (transverse to $a$). Vacuum unstrained ($n \equiv 1$).
- **G:** real gravity $g = a$, any orientation. Vacuum strained.

Then

$$
\delta_A\ (\text{horizontal}) = -\frac12\frac{a L}{c^2}, \qquad \delta_G = -\frac{a L}{c^2}.
$$

Hence $\delta_A \ne \delta_G$. The situations are locally distinguishable by a frequency-ratio measurement. The Equivalence Principle asserts they are indistinguishable. Contradiction.

The same conclusion follows from the presence versus absence of vacuum strain and co-scaling of atomic standards, which are independent of orientation.

### 8. Why GR Cannot Be Saved

GR’s geodesic equation and local-inertial-frame construction make the connection coefficients that encode “gravity” mathematically identical to those that encode pure coordinate acceleration. There is no algebraic marker inside the theory that can distinguish a real vacuum strain (which changes $\varepsilon$ and $\mu$) from a pure kinematic Doppler effect. Consequently GR cannot even formulate the distinction that experiment and the ray equation force upon us.

Higher-order post-Newtonian terms, non-local corrections, or “the EP only holds locally” do not help: the half-effect is leading-order, local (centimetre-scale), and directional under pure acceleration—precisely the regime where the EP claims equivalence.

### 9. Conclusion

Once the first-order longitudinal Doppler is treated correctly, the numerical coincidence in the vertical pure-acceleration case is understood as ordinary special-relativistic kinematics. The deeper physical distinction remains:

- Pure acceleration leaves the vacuum unstrained ($n \equiv 1$); real gravity strains it.
- Atomic standards co-scale only when the vacuum is strained.
- Pure acceleration is strongly directional (full first-order parallel, half transverse); real gravity is not.
- The clean transverse half-effect under pure acceleration has no counterpart in real gravity and is forbidden by the Equivalence Principle.

Newton’s absolute/relative distinction between acceleration and velocity is therefore preserved and extended: pure acceleration is kinematically absolute and vacuum-neutral; gravity is a genuine medium response. The Equivalence Principle erases this distinction and is false. Gravity is not spacetime curvature; it is the refractive optics of a responsive vacuum in ordinary flat Euclidean space.

**The universe is optics — even its gravity.**
